Frequently asked questions

How does syncing work?

Offmind keeps everything on your iPhone. Only groups you create and share use iCloud, so you need to be signed in to iCloud for those. Shared items update when Offmind opens or refreshes, not instantly. Your personal items are never uploaded.

How do I share with someone and leave a group?

Open Home, then People & sharing, create a group and invite a person with Apple's share sheet. They accept on their own iPhone with their Apple ID. To leave a group, open it in People & sharing and choose Leave. To remove someone, open the group and remove them. The owner can also stop sharing.

Who can I invite, and why does my link not work for someone else?

Groups use Apple's iCloud sharing and are invitation-only. The person needs an iPhone or iPad signed in to iCloud with Offmind installed; Android and web browsers can't join. Use Messages or Mail from Offmind's share sheet; other system collaboration channels depend on the device. To use WhatsApp, Snapchat or a plain link, open People & sharing, tap Invite someone, choose Another app, then select a person or group. Enter the email address or phone number of their Apple Account (with the country code) before sending. You can search the group list when you have many groups. The Add person form also offers Send a link with any app. That link only works for that Apple Account, so forwarding it to someone else will not let them in. If the link opens inside a chat app, choose Open in Safari. If they installed Offmind after tapping the link, they tap it again. If an invitation seems stuck, check your internet connection and that iCloud is on, then try again.

How do I report or block something?

In a shared group, tap the … menu next to "Added by" on an item (or long-press a shared plan) and choose Report or Hide. To block someone, open the group's member list and choose Block; their items are then hidden on your iPhone. A group owner can also remove the person, and anyone can leave a group. Unblock from the same list or from People & sharing. You can also email us at bwventures101@gmail.com with what you saw. We respond within 24 hours.

How do I back up and restore?

Open Library, the Settings gear, Data, storage & privacy, then Backup & restore. Create a backup file (optionally protected with a passphrase) and save it somewhere you trust. To restore, choose that file on the same screen. Apple Health data is not included.

How do I delete my data?

Delete items inside Offmind, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone. To remove shared items from iCloud, stop sharing or delete the group. A backup file you saved is yours to delete from Files or wherever you stored it.
