Offmind — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Offmind ("the app") is a personal mental-load manager. Your data lives on your devices and in your private iCloud — not on our servers, because we don't have any.

Data we collect

We collect nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no backend servers. Everything you enter — captures, tasks, groceries, fridge items, cards, plans, people — is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit). We never see it, and we couldn't access it if we tried.

Data shared with third parties

None. The app includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Device permissions

The app requests access to certain iOS features only to perform the functions you ask for, and that data never leaves your device except through Apple's own services:

  • Camera — scanning grocery receipts. Images are processed on-device.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — voice capture. Processed on-device.
  • Notifications — reminders for due dates, expiring items, and credits.
  • Calendar & Reminders — only if you connect them; entries stay between the app and Apple's Calendar/Reminders.
  • Contacts — only if you pick a contact when adding a friend; used solely to address the invitation.

You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings; the related features will simply stop working.

Sharing with other people

If you share a grocery list, household, or plan with someone, that sharing happens directly between your iCloud accounts via Apple's CloudKit sharing. We are not a party to it and cannot see shared content.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new revision date.
